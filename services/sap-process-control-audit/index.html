What the audit covers

Examination and evaluation of an organisation's information technology infrastructure, policies and operations.

Exhaustive auditing process to identify gaps in existing systems and infrastructure.

Review of the organisation's existing applications, documenting relative performance levels and comparing those results to industry best practices and standards.

Regular updates to stay abreast of the latest developments in technology.

SMEs

Senior business stakeholders

What you receive

  1. Interview plan
  2. As-Is analysis and key observations
  3. First-cut technical assessment report
  4. Draft SAP system assessment reports (functional and technical)
  5. Draft document on gaps and recommendations
  6. SMEs' view of key findings and correctness of findings
  7. Roadmap for different key initiatives
  8. Final report, roadmap and implementation plan
