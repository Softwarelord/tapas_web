Our 5-step support methodology

A simple, effective and tested 5 step methodology that ensures minimal time wastage through a quick decision making protocol, continuous user interaction and optimal resource allocation.

  1. Raise a callUsers can raise a call on Tapas' Dedicated Helpdesk Portal. They can upload text as well as issue related screen-shots for better representation of the issue.
  2. AssignThe issue is raised with the Support Team Head, who assigns it to the appropriate SAP consultant.
  3. AssessThe SAP consultant decides whether the issue can be resolved, or whether more information is required before resolution.
  4. Resolve
    A · Information sufficientThe consultant proceeds to resolve the issue.
    B · More information requiredThe consultant communicates with the user through the "Discussion Forum", then resolves the issue.
  5. NotifyA mail is sent to the user and the Support Team Head. A portal notification indicates successful resolution.

*SLAs and further escalations can be configured based on individual customer requirements.
